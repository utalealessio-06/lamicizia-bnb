Booking terms

Booking and payment

A booking made on this website is confirmed once payment goes through: you will immediately receive a confirmation email with your booking code.

You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay via Stripe. You can choose to pay for the whole stay or just a deposit; in that case the balance is paid at the property on arrival accepted payment methods for the balance: to be confirmed.

Cancellation

Cancellation policy to be agreed with the property: how many days before arrival you can cancel free of charge, whether the deposit is refunded, what happens in case of no-show.

Check-in and check-out

  • Check-in from 12:00 to 20:00. For other times, please let us know in advance.
  • Check-out by 10:30.

Pets

Small and medium-sized pets are welcome by prior arrangement: please mention it in the booking notes.
