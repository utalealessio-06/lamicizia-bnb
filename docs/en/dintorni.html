Get directions

By car from Rome

Take the Via Cassia (SS2) towards Viterbo, then follow signs for Montefiascone. Alternatively, take the A1 motorway to Orte, the expressway to Viterbo, then the Cassia north.

By car from Florence and the north

A1 motorway, exit Orvieto, then the main road towards Montefiascone and Lake Bolsena.

By train or plane

The handiest railway stations are Orvieto (Rome–Florence line) and Viterbo; the nearest airport is Rome Fiumicino, 84 km away. Message us before you travel and we will help you plan the last leg.
